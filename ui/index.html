Welcome to WoWSims - Forever! This is a community-driven project to provide class and raid simulations for World of Warcraft®: Forever, the Classic+ relaunch announced at BlizzCon 2026. It is an unofficial fork of wowsims/classic, not the official Forever sim, carrying Forever's talent trees, races and ruleset through every sim.

Forever has no beta client yet, so every talent, rank and coefficient here was read off a BlizzCon 2026 demo tooltip, and the demo mostly showed rank 1. The rest is assumed, counted line by line in the beta re-verification checklist. Treat every number these sims produce as provisional.
